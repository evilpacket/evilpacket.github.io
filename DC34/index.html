⚠ DC34-COMPLIANT SAO PORTS ONLY. This badge expects power on the right (art view). Mirrored or older 1.69bis-era hosts with power on the left feed it backwards.

How to

TAP A HORN

right = next pattern · left = previous. The mouth flashes the pattern number for a beat, then it plays.

HOLD BOTH HORNS

Hold ≈1s: a mouth LED points at a mode. Keep holding to cycle, release to choose. It double-flashes your pick.
